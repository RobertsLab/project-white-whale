## Research Themes (from the retrieved corpus)

- **Environmental epigenetics** — thermal/tidal-level methylation (Wang 2021, 2023), ocean acidification (Venkataraman 2022), pesticide/diuron transgenerational effects (Sol Dourdin, Akcha, Rondon, Sussarellu)
- **Development & reproduction** — methylome dynamics through development (Riviere 2017), male gametes (Olson 2014), sex determination in gonads (Sun 2022), single-cell germ-cell specification (Gavery 2025)
- **Immunity / disease** — WGBS of Vibrio response (Li 2024), POMS methylation (Valdivieso 2025), immune priming (multiple *Fish Shellfish Immunol* / *Front Immunol*)
- **Foundational methylomes** — first oyster methylation maps (Gavery 2010; Wang 2014, PRJNA173440)
- **Cross-species / stress transcriptomics** — acid response (Wang 2025), OA reaction norms (Lutier 2025), hypoxia+thermal (Kim 2025)
