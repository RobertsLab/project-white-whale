    Genes

    Every library is scored over the same genes of the reference annotation, so studies can be compared gene by gene. A gene's value is its CpG methylation averaged over the libraries with at least five covered CpGs in it. Descriptive only; each card says whether CpGs on C/T SNPs were masked.
